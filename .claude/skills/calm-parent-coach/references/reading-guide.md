# Reading guide: No-Drama Discipline (Siegel & Bryson)

What to read, and when. Chapter titles are from the book; page numbers vary by edition.

## Chapter by chapter

**Ch 1 - "ReDISCIPLINE-ing Discipline"**
- Discipline = teaching, not punishing.
- The Why? What? How? questions: Why did my child do this? What do I want to teach? How do I teach it best?
- Two goals: cooperation now, and building the brain for the long run.
- Read when: you're unsure what discipline should look like, or feel stuck in punishment mode.

**Ch 2 - "Your Brain on Discipline"**
- Upstairs brain (thinking) vs downstairs brain (upset).
- The brain changes with experience: "neurons that fire together wire together."
- Read when: you wonder why your child can't "just listen" in big moments.

**Ch 3 - "From Tantrum to Tranquility: Connection Is the Key"**
- Why connection comes first. It calms the reactive, upset brain.
- Read when: meltdowns, tantrums, wild moments.

**Ch 4 - "No-Drama Connection in Action"**
- How to connect: calm body language, get below eye level, validate feelings, stop talking and listen, reflect back what you hear.
- Read when: you don't know what to do in the first 30 seconds.

**Ch 5 - "1-2-3 Discipline: Redirecting for Today and Tomorrow"**
- 1 definition, 2 principles, 3 outcomes. Summary in `chapter-5-123-discipline.md`.
- Read when: planning the calm teaching talk.

**Ch 6 - "Addressing Behavior: As Simple as R-E-D-I-R-E-C-T"**
- **R**educe words
- **E**mbrace emotions
- **D**escribe, don't preach
- **I**nvolve your child
- **R**eframe a no into a conditional yes
- **E**mphasize the positive
- **C**reatively approach the situation
- **T**each mindsight tools
- Read when: you need specific tools for repeat behaviors.

**Conclusion - "Four Messages of Hope"**
- You don't have to be perfect. Repair after mistakes.
- Read when: you feel you've failed.

**Back-of-book extras**
- **"Connect and Redirect" refrigerator sheet:** put one in both homes.
- **"Notes to Our Kids' Caregivers":** good to share with grandparents and other caregivers.
- **"Twenty Discipline Mistakes Even Great Parents Make":** a quick self-check.

## Problem -> what to read

| Problem | Read |
|---|---|
| Wild / hyper moments | Ch 3 + Ch 6 (Creatively approach, Reduce words) |
| Won't listen | Ch 2 + Ch 4 |
| Repeats the same misbehavior | Ch 5 + Ch 6 (Teach mindsight tools) |
| Trouble at grandparents' or with other caregivers | Caregivers note + Ch 4 |
| Parent lost their temper | Conclusion + repair |
| Parents doing it differently in two homes | Ch 5 (consistent, not rigid) + refrigerator sheet |

## Busy-parent plan

- One short section a week. That's enough.
- Both parents read the **same** section that week, so you can talk about it.
- Pick the section that matches the current challenge in the Progress tracker.
